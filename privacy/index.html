Privacy Policy

Effective June 8, 2026

JAReferee LLC ("JAReferee," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to all tools and services provided by JAReferee, including JAssignor, the Gameday Command Center, the Evaluator, and the JAReferee marketing site at jareferee.com.

1. Who We Are

JAReferee LLC is a Colorado limited liability company. We build software tools that support soccer referee assignment, gameday operations, evaluation, and development. Our tools are used by state soccer associations, tournament directors, referee assignors, referee coaches, and the referees themselves.

2. Information We Collect

Information you or your association provide

  • Name, mobile phone number, and email address
  • Referee certification level, badge year, and association affiliations
  • Tournament and game assignment history
  • Availability information you choose to share
  • Optional profile details such as preferred name or pronunciation

Information generated through use of the tools

  • Check-in records (time, venue, field, game)
  • Game assignment status (assigned, accepted, declined, completed, released)
  • Switches and re-assignments
  • Evaluation and coaching feedback recorded about your performance
  • Message delivery logs for SMS and email sent on your behalf

Technical information

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate location based on IP
  • Page views and basic usage analytics for our own service improvement

We do not collect Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, payment card information, or bank account numbers. Payment to referees is processed by a separate third-party provider (ArbiterPay) and JAReferee does not store or have access to payment account details.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send game assignment notifications and schedule updates
  • Send pre-game reminders and weather or logistics alerts
  • Coordinate switches when a referee cannot work an assigned game
  • Record check-ins at venues and fields
  • Generate referee evaluation records and developmental feedback
  • Produce pay reports for export to ArbiterPay for payment processing
  • Improve our tools, fix bugs, and analyze usage in aggregate
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about service changes

4. Mobile Phone Numbers and Text Messaging

JAReferee will not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

If you have provided your mobile number to your state soccer association or to JAReferee directly, you may receive transactional SMS messages from JAReferee related to your active officiating commitments. These include game assignments, schedule updates, switch alerts, pre-game reminders, and weather or logistics announcements.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies by tournament activity and your assignment volume. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may request help by replying HELP. You may also opt out by emailing support@jareferee.com or by completing the SMS Opt-Out Letter provided at tournament registration.

Refs who opt out of SMS will receive assignments by email instead and may check in manually at venues.

5. How We Share Information

We share your information only in the following limited cases:

  • With your state soccer association when JAReferee is used to support that association's referee program. Examples include the Colorado Soccer Association.
  • With tournament directors and assignors for tournaments you have accepted assignments at.
  • With referee coaches who provide evaluation and developmental feedback at events where you are working.
  • With ArbiterPay for the purpose of processing your tournament pay. Information shared is limited to what ArbiterPay requires for payout (name, ArbiterPay ID where provided, and the dollar amount earned).
  • With service providers who help us operate the platform, including Twilio for SMS delivery, Google Workspace for email, and GitHub Pages for site hosting. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality.
  • When required by law in response to a valid legal process or to protect rights, property, or safety.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as you remain active in a referee program that uses JAReferee tools, plus a reasonable period afterward for audit, tax, dispute resolution, and association recordkeeping purposes. Tournament records may be retained indefinitely for historical and statistical purposes by the relevant association. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting support@jareferee.com, subject to legal and association recordkeeping obligations.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect information in transit and at rest, including HTTPS for all web traffic, encrypted storage with our service providers, and role-based access controls within JAReferee. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password if you create an account, and notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorized access.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and association recordkeeping obligations
  • Opt out of SMS messages (reply STOP, or email support@jareferee.com)
  • Opt out of marketing email (none is currently sent, but you may request a written confirmation)

To exercise these rights, email support@jareferee.com. We will respond within a reasonable time frame and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Soccer referees may be under the age of 18, and some are under the age of 13. JAReferee tools are operated through state soccer associations and tournament organizations that hold the primary relationship with young referees and their parents or guardians. Where a referee is under 13, registration and consent must be provided by a parent or legal guardian through the state association or tournament. JAReferee does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 outside of this association-mediated process. If you believe we have collected information from a child without proper consent, please contact support@jareferee.com and we will take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, when appropriate, notify users by email or in-app notice. Continued use of JAReferee tools after the effective date of a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

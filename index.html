One Saturday, start to finish.

This is the order it happens, and who sees what.

6:50
The referee opens Your day

Games synced from the assigning platform overnight. Countdown, field, crew, the rules for that league, and a map to the venue. In Spanish if that is what they chose.

7:14
One tap to check in

No QR hunt, no clipboard. The name turns green on the board. Not checked in turns gold 30 minutes before kickoff and red at 15.

7:40
Running late, with an answer

The referee taps Running late and a time. The coordinator acknowledges from the board, and the gap stays under Needs attention until it is covered.

10:30
A coach watches and writes

The coach's phone shows every game at the venue. One note per referee, written in the moment, reviewed by the program before release.

7:52 pm
The note lands the same night

On the referee's phone, on their card, in their language. The program sees it was read. Next weekend's coach builds on it.

Three weeks to your first live Saturday.

Colorado was the first. The next state starts from everything it taught us.

Week 1

Your people

Export your referees from the US Soccer Learning Center. Names, licenses, clearances and contacts load into a database under your own account. Minors are handled under your protection rules from day one.

Week 2

Your games

Connect your assigning platform. Games sync automatically, every group, weeks ahead. Referee names are matched to people once and remembered.

Week 3

Your weekend

Coordinators, coaches and reviewers get their unlocks. Pick one venue for the first Saturday. By Sunday night you have your first coverage numbers.

A new way to run a referee program

What's now possible.

Every referee carries a set of tools into a game. A whistle, a card, their presence, strong body language. Ref coaches help referees sharpen those tools. JAReferee is the platform underneath the whole program. The laws of the game, the club and tournament education, the ref coach's full kit, the gameday command center, and the academy that brings the next generation in. Every piece talks to every other piece.

Eyes on the match, always.
Coach during the game. Write afterward. Never both at once. The Ref Coach tools capture every foul, every offside call, every key moment with a single tap, so your eyes stay on the field where they belong. Every detail is waiting for you when the final whistle blows.
One command center for the whole day.
Game switches. Crew changes. Weather calls. Field reassignments. Check-in. The Gameday Command Center keeps every stakeholder on the same screen, in real time, from the first whistle to the last referee's email.
Education at the front of the pipeline.
The Reference Library puts the laws in every referee's pocket. Teams educates clubs, coaches, players, and parents on the game and the people who officiate it. The Academy trains the next instructors, the next site coordinators, the next ref coaches.
Better notes. Better debriefs. Better referees.
That's the job. Developing referees who handle bigger games, more pressure, more responsibility. Having the right tools shortens the path. JAReferee is built to be the toolkit underneath everything else you bring to the field.

Why we built it

Referees first.

JAReferee is mobile first, for the touchline. Every tool scales cleanly to a laptop or tablet when you sit down to review, write up, or run the day. Each referee receives their own personalized report after the match. Their game, their coaching remarks, their development notes, delivered cleanly by email when you're done.

No notes lost on a phone. No notebooks ruined by sunscreen. No fragmented tools that don't talk to each other. More time on the field watching and coaching. More referees reached. Better referees developed.

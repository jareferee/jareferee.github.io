A new tool for the toolkit

What's now possible.

Every referee carries a set of tools with them into a game. A whistle, a card, their presence, strong body language. The best referees have learned to use which tool, and when. Ref Coaches help referees sharpen those tools. JAReferee is a new tool for the Ref Coach's toolkit. The right tool for the job, at the right time, to help develop better referees.

Eyes on the match, always.
Coach during the game. Writer afterward. Never both at once. JAReferee captures every foul, every offside call, every key moment with a single tap, so your eyes stay on the field where they belong. Every detail is waiting for you when the final whistle blows.
Organized notes, structured debriefs.
Walk into the debrief with every key moment time stamped, sorted, and ready to discuss. The crew sees the same event log and stats you do. Conversations get sharper. Narratives get easier to write. Your expertise stays at the center. JAReferee just hands you the organized material to work with.
Built for the long Saturday.
Two games. Three games. A full tournament weekend. Every match logged on the same phone, every crew evaluated cleanly. No cramped hands, no soaked notebooks, no shuffled pages. Just the next game ready when you are.
Better notes. Better debriefs. Better referees.
That's the job. Developing referees who handle bigger games, more pressure, and more responsibility. Having the right tools shortens the path. JAReferee is built to be part of that toolkit, alongside your eye, your knowledge, and everything else you bring to the field.
Why we built it

Referees first.

JAReferee was built mobile first, for the touchline. It scales cleanly to your laptop or tablet when you sit down to review and write up. Each crew member receives their own personalized PDF after the match. Their game, their coaching remarks, their development notes, delivered by email when you're done. A copy goes to you, the Ref Coach, for long term tracking and pattern recognition across a season.

No notes lost on a phone. No notebooks ruined by sunscreen. More time on the field watching and coaching. More referees reached. Better referees developed.

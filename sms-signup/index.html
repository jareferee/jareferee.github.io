SMS Sign-Up

Sign up to receive text messages from JAReferee about your game assignments, schedule, and gameday logistics.

What you will receive

  • Game assignment notifications when you are added to a crew
  • Schedule updates and switch alerts
  • Pre-game reminders one hour before kickoff
  • Weather and venue logistics announcements

You will not receive marketing messages. JAReferee sends only operational messages tied to your active officiating commitments.

Help and Opt-Out

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any JAReferee message. You can also email support@jareferee.com or complete the SMS Opt-Out Letter provided at tournament registration. Refs who opt out receive assignments by email and check in manually instead.

For help, reply HELP to any JAReferee message or email support@jareferee.com.

Cost

JAReferee does not charge for SMS messages. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

Privacy

JAReferee will not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for more.
